Welcome to Mixology.

How well do you know your colors?

  1. One color name a day. Same name for everyone. You only see the color at the reveal.
  2. Mix it from five paints. Pick a pigment, then tap or hold the pot to add it.
  3. Seal two attempts. Your best one is scored by how close it lands. 85%+ beats par.

How to play

Every day has one color, given only as a name. Nobody shows you the color — mix what you think the name looks like.

Pick a pigment from the ring, pick a tool, then dose the pot in the middle. Seal two attempts — the round ends after the second, and your best attempt is your score.

The pot

The pot is always 100% full — it starts as pure white paint. Every drop you add pushes the other paints down to make room, so the total never goes above 100%. One drop is 1% of the pot. And a messed-up pot can always be rescued: just keep adding the color you want and it will take over.

The tools

Dropper — 1 drop per click on the pot.

Pour Bucket — press and hold the pot to pour, about 30 drops per second. Let go to stop.

Undo takes back your last press or pour in one step; Reset pot starts the pot over.

On a keyboard: 1–5 pick red, yellow, blue, black, white · D Dropper · P Pour Bucket · Z Undo · Enter on the pot adds paint · PgUp/PgDn change month in the calendar.

Scoring

Your score is % accuracy: 100% means your mix is indistinguishable from the official color, measured by perceptual distance (CIEDE2000). 85% or better beats par.

A fresh color arrives every day — same name for everyone. Come back tomorrow.

After the round

The reveal shows the official recipe next to your mix. Share result sends your score without naming the color, so no spoilers. On phones it attaches an image card. Copy duel link lets a friend try to beat you on the same color.
